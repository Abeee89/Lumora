# Target Users

> Status: Proposal, pending approval.

## Primary segment for MVP
**University students and exam-prep students.** They have multiple subjects, fixed deadlines (exams), and a strong need for planned-vs-actual visibility. Other segments are served incidentally by the same features.

## Segments

### 1. High-school students
- **Needs:** simple daily routine, subject balance, parent/teacher-independent self-discipline, exam countdowns.
- **Pain points:** distraction on phones, no sense of how much they really studied, overwhelmed before tests.
- **Notes:** may be minors → privacy and consent concerns (see ASSUMPTIONS-AND-RISKS.md). Not the MVP focus.

### 2. University students (MVP primary)
- **Needs:** per-course hour tracking, balancing many subjects, exam schedules, notes per topic, weekly/monthly view.
- **Pain points:** several courses compete for time, cramming before exams, scattered notes, no visibility on neglected courses.

### 3. Exam candidates (certification / entrance exams) (MVP primary)
- **Needs:** syllabus as topic checklist, countdown, readiness measure, revision cycles.
- **Pain points:** large syllabus, unclear progress, anxiety about coverage, no revision scheduling.

### 4. Self-directed learners / working professionals
- **Needs:** flexible plans without a fixed curriculum, consistency, short sessions, minimal setup.
- **Pain points:** irregular time, loses habit after missed days, no external accountability.

## Cross-segment needs
| Need | Segments |
|---|---|
| Know how long I studied per subject | all |
| Daily target and progress | all |
| Distraction-free timer | all |
| Topic-level progress and notes | university, exam, self-directed |
| Exam countdown and checklist | high-school, university, exam |
| Consistency/habit feedback | self-directed, high-school |
| Accountability with peers | high-school, university (Phase 3) |
