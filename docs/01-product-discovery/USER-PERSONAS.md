# User Personas

> Fictional personas for planning; not research-validated. Status: Proposal.

## 1. Rani — Exam-prep student (MVP primary)
- **Profile:** 19, preparing for a national university entrance exam in 14 weeks.
- **Goals:** cover the full syllabus, know weak subjects, hit 4 h/day.
- **Frustrations:** syllabus is huge; no idea if she is "on track"; keeps losing paper checklists.
- **Devices:** phone (timer), laptop (planning, notes).
- **Key features:** Exam Management (countdown, topic checklist), Daily Study Goals, Pomodoro Timer, Study Hours by Subject, Statistics.
- **Quote:** "I don't need more motivation, I need to know if I'm actually behind."

## 2. Dimas — University student with multi-subject load (MVP primary)
- **Profile:** 21, 6 courses, part-time job.
- **Goals:** balance courses, avoid last-week cramming, keep notes per course.
- **Frustrations:** one demanding course eats all time; scattered notes; midterms sneak up.
- **Devices:** laptop in class/library, phone for timer.
- **Key features:** Subjects/Topics, To-Do & Priority, Calendar heatmap, Notes, Monthly Statistics.
- **Quote:** "I thought I studied physics a lot — turns out it was 3 hours this month."

## 3. Maya — Working professional, self-learner
- **Profile:** 30, learning data engineering after work, 45–90 min/day, irregular.
- **Goals:** build a consistent habit, finish a self-chosen curriculum.
- **Frustrations:** loses momentum after a missed week; no external structure.
- **Devices:** laptop evenings, phone on commute.
- **Key features:** Stopwatch, Daily Goals, (Phase 2) Streaks, reminders, flashcards.
- **Quote:** "A bad week shouldn't feel like starting over."

## 4. Arif — Study-group organizer (Phase 3 driver)
- **Profile:** 22, runs a 6-person study circle for a professional certification.
- **Goals:** keep group on schedule, share progress, friendly competition.
- **Frustrations:** chat-based accountability is noisy; no shared view of effort.
- **Devices:** phone and laptop.
- **Key features:** Study Groups, Leaderboards, shared exam dates (all Phase 3); individual MVP features meanwhile.
- **Quote:** "Everyone says they studied; I'd like numbers we all agree on."
