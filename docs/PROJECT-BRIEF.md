# Project Brief — Lumora

> Status: Phase 1 proposal, pending approval. Details in `docs/01-product-discovery/`.

**Vision:** One loop for learners — plan → study (timer) → record → review → revise — with planned-vs-actual tracking. ([PRODUCT-VISION](01-product-discovery/PRODUCT-VISION.md))

**Users:** Primary: university and exam-prep students. Also high-school students and self-directed learners. ([TARGET-USERS](01-product-discovery/TARGET-USERS.md), [USER-PERSONAS](01-product-discovery/USER-PERSONAS.md), [USER-JOURNEYS](01-product-discovery/USER-JOURNEYS.md))

**MVP:** Auth/profile, subjects & topics, hours tracking by subject, Pomodoro/Stopwatch, daily goals, to-dos with priority, read-only activity calendar, daily/monthly statistics, basic notes, basic exam management, export. ([PRODUCT-SCOPE](01-product-discovery/PRODUCT-SCOPE.md))

**Phases:**
- Phase 2: drag-and-drop calendar, revision/flashcards, streak then XP, ambient audio, exam readiness, reminders, PWA.
- Phase 3: AI schedule, study groups & leaderboards, public REST API.
([FEATURE-ANALYSIS](01-product-discovery/FEATURE-ANALYSIS.md), [FEATURE-PRIORITIZATION](FEATURE-PRIORITIZATION.md))

**Constraints:** web-only responsive, single-user MVP, English, free, no native apps/payments/chat. Business rules: [BUSINESS-RULES](01-product-discovery/BUSINESS-RULES.md).

**Success metrics:** D7 retention ≥ 25%; ≥ 4 sessions/week/active user; daily goal met ≥ 40% of active days; activation ≥ 50%.

**Risks:** see [ASSUMPTIONS-AND-RISKS](01-product-discovery/ASSUMPTIONS-AND-RISKS.md).
