# PHASE 1 — PRODUCT DISCOVERY & REQUIREMENT ANALYSIS

You are acting as a Senior Product Manager, Product Analyst, and Software Architect.

We are planning a comprehensive Study Tracker  Learning Management & Personal Productivity Web Application.

The application will help students and independent learners organize their learning activities, track study progress, manage learning materials, prepare for exams, and improve productivity.

## Important Instructions

- Work in PLANNING MODE only.
- Do not write application code.
- Do not create database migrations.
- Do not install dependencies.
- Do not implement UI components.
- Focus exclusively on product discovery and requirement analysis.
- Do not assume that every proposed feature must be included in the MVP.
- Identify ambiguities, dependencies, risks, and conflicting requirements.
- Make reasonable recommendations and document assumptions.
- Ask questions only when a decision materially affects product direction or architecture.

## Proposed Core Features

Analyze the following features

1. Study Activity Calendar
2. Study Hours Tracking by Subject
3. Daily Study Goals
4. Pomodoro Timer and Stopwatch
5. Daily and Monthly Statistics
6. Notes and Revision Tools
7. Drag-and-Drop Study Calendar
8. AI-Generated Study Schedule
9. Focus Timer and Ambient Audio
10. Habit Streak and XP Gamification
11. Exam Preparation Management
12. Study Groups and Leaderboards
13. REST API for External Integrations
14. To-Do List and Priority Management

You may recommend additional features that improve the product.

## Tasks

### 1. Product Understanding
- Define the product vision.
- Define the main problem being solved.
- Identify target users and user personas.
- Identify user needs and pain points.
- Define the product's unique value proposition.

### 2. Feature Analysis
For every proposed feature
- Explain its purpose.
- Identify its main functionality.
- Identify its dependencies.
- Identify its complexity.
- Identify its technical risks.
- Recommend whether it belongs in MVP, Phase 2, or Phase 3.

### 3. User Journey
Describe the expected user journey
- First-time user onboarding.
- Creating subjects and topics.
- Creating study plans.
- Starting and completing study sessions.
- Recording study progress.
- Reviewing statistics.
- Preparing for examinations.

### 4. Product Scope
Define
- In-scope functionality.
- Out-of-scope functionality.
- MVP boundaries.
- Future expansion opportunities.

### 5. Business Rules
Identify important product rules, including
- Planned study time versus actual study time.
- Study session completion.
- Daily goal calculations.
- XP earning rules.
- Habit streak calculations.
- Exam preparation progress.
- Study schedule conflict handling.

### 6. Risks and Recommendations
Identify
- Product risks.
- Technical risks.
- UX risks.
- Scalability considerations.
- Features that may be unnecessarily complex.
