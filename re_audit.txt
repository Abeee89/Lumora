# LUMORA — FINAL DOCUMENTATION READINESS AUDIT

Perform a strict read-only audit of the corrected Lumora documentation.

## Sources

Inspect the complete current documentation from Phase 1 through Phase 8, including all ADRs, governance documents, database design, UI/UX, API contracts, roadmap, milestones, testing strategy, and the approved Owner Decision Record.

## Audit objectives

1. Verify every Critical finding C-1 through C-6.
2. Verify every Major finding M-1 through M-31.
3. Verify every Supplemental finding S-1 through S-5.
4. Verify M00-A through M00-F specifications and acceptance criteria.
5. Verify every approved decision is reflected consistently in all affected documents.
6. Verify all requirement-to-design-to-implementation-to-test traceability.
7. Verify migration order, module ownership, dependency graph, and milestone gates.
8. Verify no unresolved global blocker is incorrectly classified as a minor issue.

## Readiness rules

Use only these statuses:

- PASS
- PARTIAL
- FAIL
- NOT APPLICABLE

A finding is PASS only when its resolution is evidenced in the actual documentation.

Do not count a heading, reference, or generic statement as proof of implementation readiness.

Do not treat planned tests as executed tests.

Do not treat a proposed ADR as an accepted ADR.

## Required reports

- `docs/FINAL-ARCHITECTURE-REVIEW.md`
- `docs/IMPLEMENTATION-READINESS.md`
- `docs/TRACEABILITY-MATRIX.md`
- `docs/OPEN-DECISIONS.md`

Also produce a concise audit summary with:

- Resolved findings
- Partially resolved findings
- Unresolved findings
- Global blockers
- Milestone-specific blockers
- M00 authorization prerequisites
- Build Mode authorization recommendation

## Restrictions

Read-only audit. Do not modify documentation, code, dependencies, migrations, or configuration.

Do not authorize M00 or Build Mode.

Stop and wait for explicit owner approval.
