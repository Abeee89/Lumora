For every table, specify:

- Description.

## 5. Database Rules

Pay special attention to:

### Study Plans vs Study Sessions
A planned study activity and an actual completed study session must be separate records.

### Timer and Study Session
Define how timer state is persisted and how duplicate or overlapping sessions are prevented.

### XP
Use an XP transaction ledger instead of relying only on a mutable total XP value.

### Historical Data
Define what happens to historical records when a subject, topic, or task is deleted.

### Time Zones
Define how timestamps and study dates are stored and calculated.

### Statistics
Identify which metrics are calculated dynamically and which may be aggregated.

### Study Groups
Define ownership, membership, visibility, and access constraints.

## 6. Indexing and Performance

Recommend indexes for:
- Study session queries.
- Calendar queries.
- Subject statistics.
- Task priority and status.
- Flashcard revision.
- Group leaderboard.
- Search.
- User-specific queries.

## 7. Backup and Data Integrity

- Data export.
- Referential integrity.

- Data retention.
